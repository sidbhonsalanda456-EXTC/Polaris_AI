ENERGY CONVERSION

One source.
Two useful outputs.

CHP is different from a normal generator because the system can make electricity while also recovering useful heat instead of wasting it.

01

Energy Input

The CHP unit receives its available fuel or energy input and converts it inside the cogeneration system.

02

Electricity

Part of the converted energy is used to produce electrical power for station loads.

03

Useful Heat

Recoverable heat can support heating-related station needs instead of being completely wasted.
