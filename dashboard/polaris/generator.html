Generator Operation

Controlled Backup Power

The generator is not the first choice for normal energy supply. POLARIS attempts to satisfy demand through renewable sources and stored energy first, then uses the generator when additional power is required.

01 • START
▶

Automatic Start

If available renewable generation and storage cannot satisfy the required demand, the optimizer can call the generator for backup power.
