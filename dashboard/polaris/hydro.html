THE ENERGY JOURNEY

From Water to Electricity

Instead of a normal dashboard, this section shows the physical journey of energy through the hydropower system.

01

Water Flow

Available water flow provides the energy input for the modeled hydropower resource.

02

Turbine

Moving water drives the turbine mechanism, converting flow energy into mechanical rotation.

03

Generator

The rotating system produces electrical energy for the station.

04

Station Load

Electricity is supplied to station demand according to the POLARIS optimizer.

RESILIENCE MODE

What Happens During a Flood?

POLARIS is designed to respond automatically when a natural disaster changes the availability of an energy source.

⚠

Flood Scenario

In the modeled flood scenario from 04:00–04:59, hydropower is derated to 10% of its normal modeled capacity.

↗

Automatic Compensation

The optimizer can shift energy supply toward other available renewable sources, battery storage and backup resources.

✦ AI + Hydropower Intelligence

If the system predicts that hydropower will become unavailable or significantly reduced, the AI-assisted optimizer can prepare the energy system in advance. Battery reserve can be protected and other renewable sources can be prioritized.
