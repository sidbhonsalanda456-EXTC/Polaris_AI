PASSIVE DESIGN

The building
becomes the energy system.

Passive solar does not work like a traditional generator. It lowers energy demand by making better use of sunlight, heat retention and building design.

☀

Solar Gain

Building orientation and glazing can capture available sunlight and turn it into useful indoor warmth.

◈

Insulation

Strong insulation helps keep heat inside the station instead of allowing it to escape into the extreme polar environment.

≋

Thermal Mass

Materials can store useful heat and release it gradually, reducing sudden heating demand.

◇

Smart Envelope

Windows, walls and building surfaces work together to reduce unnecessary energy loss.
