Solar Energy — Explained Simply

What the numbers on this page actually mean.

☀ Why Solar Matters

Solar is the first renewable source in the POLARIS priority system. When solar power is available, the optimizer tries to use it before moving to lower-priority sources.

  • Clean electricity
  • No fuel required while generating
  • Can directly power station loads
  • Extra energy can support battery charging

⚡ What Happens When Solar Drops?

Solar generation is not constant. If sunlight becomes unavailable, POLARIS does not simply stop the station.

  • Another renewable source can be selected
  • Battery can provide stored energy
  • Backup generation can be used if required
  • Critical loads remain the highest priority
