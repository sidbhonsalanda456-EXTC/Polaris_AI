AI DECISION ENGINE

How POLARIS decides.

The AI does not blindly select a backup. It evaluates the complete station condition before changing the energy source.

Decision sequence

01
Detect

Check renewable generation, source failures and station demand.

02
Predict

Look ahead for upcoming deficits, unavailable sources and demand changes.

03
Protect

Keep critical loads supplied and preserve battery reserve.

04
Dispatch

Select the best available backup source for the current condition.

What can trigger a shift?

⚡
Renewable shortage

Available renewable generation falls below station demand.

!
Source failure

Wind, hydro or another source becomes unavailable.

↓
Low battery SOC

Battery reserve approaches its minimum operating threshold.

☄
Disaster condition

Flood, tsunami or storm changes resource availability.
