EMERGENCY RESPONSE

Detect.
Adapt. Protect.

The disaster-management layer works together with the AI optimizer instead of operating as a separate system.

PHASE 01

DETECT

The system identifies a disaster condition or source degradation through incoming station and resource data.

  • Monitor source status
  • Detect derating
  • Check station demand
  • Identify critical loads
PHASE 02

ADAPT

AI recalculates the energy balance and selects the best available combination of renewable generation and storage.

  • Recalculate supply
  • Shift energy source
  • Preserve battery reserve
  • Pre-charge when possible
PHASE 03

PROTECT

Critical station operations remain the highest priority. Non-critical demand can be reduced during severe shortages.

  • Heating protected
  • Communication protected
  • Medical protected
  • Shed non-critical loads
