⚠

Storm Response

In the modeled storm scenario, wind output can be reduced to 15%. POLARIS automatically compensates using available renewable sources, battery storage and backup systems.

🌬️ Renewable Priority

Wind is the second priority renewable source after Solar in the POLARIS energy optimization sequence.

🔄 Automatic Switching

If wind becomes unavailable, AUTO MODE can shift the station toward other available renewable sources and storage.

🛡️ Safety First

When wind exceeds the modeled cut-out limit, the turbine stops and the optimizer protects station energy supply through alternative sources.
